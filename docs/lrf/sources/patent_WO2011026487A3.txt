## ABSTRACT
Abstract
The invention relates to an adjusting method and an adjusting device for adjusting an axis (2.3) of a shooting simulator (2) to the line of sight (1.2) of the sighting apparatus (1.1) of a firearm (1). The adjusting device is composed of two modules, i.e. a display module (3) having an axis (3.3) and a camera module (4) having an axis (4.3). The axis (3.3) of the display module can be adjusted so as to be aligned with the line of sight (1.2) by moving crosshairs on the display (3.1). In order to align the axis (2.3) of the simulator with the previously adjusted axis (3.3) of the display module, and thus with the line of sight (1.2), a laser beam emitted by the shooting simulator is represented on the camera chip and is moved into a position that is adequate to the crosshairs by actuating a tilting mechanism which is part of the shooting simulator (2).
