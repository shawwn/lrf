## ABSTRACT
Abstract
 Translated from German
 The invention relates to a laser rangefinder and a method for measuring the distance of a targeted target (3), operating on the principle of pulse transit time measurement, in which the optoelectronic receiver (4) protected from hitting destructive, at the target (3) reflected radiation energy components of the emitted laser pulses becomes. The rangefinder has two laser radiation sources (1), (8) of different pulse energy. Only when a laser pulse with the low pulse energy leads to no evaluable received signal, a laser pulse of high pulse energy is emitted.
