 The present invention relates to a distance measuring device.
 From the publication 
 EP 0 468 302 A2 a distance measuring device is known. This distance measuring device is an interferometric device, with the ball lens acting as a resonator (multiple reflections or standing waves inside the ball). This would not work well, ie there would be no sufficiently pronounced interference pattern (which the device described there requires to function) if the ball lens used there were highly transmissive (in the relevant wavelength range).
 The 
 US 2002/0018217 A1 and the 
 WO 2010/108365 A1 disclose an optical beam shaping unit for shaping a beam bundle, the optical beam shaping unit having at least one spherical lens for shaping the bundle of rays, the spherical lens allowing a majority of the light incident on the spherical lens to pass through, and the optical beam shaping unit having at least one optical unit with a positive effective focal length , which is arranged in a beam path with the spherical lens.
 Against this background, the present invention provides an improved distance measuring device according to the main claim. Advantageous embodiments result from the subclaims and the following description.
 An optical beam shaping unit for shaping a beam is presented. The optical beam shaping unit has at least one spherical lens for shaping the beam, the spherical lens allowing a majority of the light incident on the spherical lens to pass through, and the optical beam shaping unit having at least one optical unit with a positive effective focal length, which is arranged in a beam path with the spherical lens is.
 An optical beam shaping unit can be understood to mean a device for the optical deflection or refraction of a beam. A spherical lens can be understood to mean an essentially spherical lens. The Ball lens can be designed to image the beam from an object plane to an image plane. For example, the ball lens can be made of glass or a transparent plastic. In particular, the ball lens can be made from a sapphire material with a high refractive index. The spherical lens can, for example, be a mass product for fiber coupling, such as is used for lens connectors for connecting optical fibers. The ball lens can, for example, be part of a laser diode collimator of the optical beam shaping unit. The spherical lens should let the main part, ie at least half of the light impinging on the spherical lens, pass through, ie be transparent or transmitting for this part of the light. The spherical lens is preferably transparent for at least three quarters of the light incident on the spherical lens; in particular, the spherical lens should be transparent or transmissive for at least eighty percent or even more favorably for at least ninety percent of the light incident on the spherical lens. The optical unit can be a unit with a positive effective focal length. For example, the optical unit can be designed to collimate the beam and / or the optical unit can be, for example, a lens, a lens system or a mirror. The design of the lens is designed (layer design) to transmit the maximum amount of light that is physically possible (limits are the Fresnel losses).
 The approach described here is based on the knowledge that by using spherical optics to form a beam within an optical beam shaping unit, an overall length of the optical beam shaping unit can be minimized. Another advantage is the simple variability of the overall length and focal length as well as a simplified adjustment. The fact that expensive lenses with a correspondingly expensive frame and assembly can be dispensed with and simple frame technology such as burr grips can be used also significantly reduce the production costs of the optical beam shaping unit.
 According to the invention, the distance measuring device comprises at least one light source for generating the beam. The spherical lens is designed to image the beam of rays generated by the light source on the optical element. A light source can be understood to mean, for example, a light-emitting diode or a laser diode. Here, the ball lens is in a beam path of the beam between the optical unit and the Light source arranged. For example, a distance between the ball lens and the light source or a distance between the ball lens and the optical unit can be varied. In this way, for example in the case of an optical beam shaping unit installed in a distance measuring device, the resulting focal length of the arrangement can be varied, as a result of which a change in the divergence of a transmission or reception channel is brought about. The performance (such as the range) of the distance measuring device can be improved in this way. The spherical lens maps the source (laser diode or APD, ...) into an intermediate image plane, which represents the focal plane of the optical element. In addition to other factors, the improved range essentially depends on the divergence of the laser beam, which is influenced by distance variation.
 The optical unit can be designed as a converging lens, mirror and / or lens system. In particular, the optical unit can be designed, for example, as a plano-convex converging lens. Such an optical unit can be provided particularly inexpensively. The plano-convex lens is usually an aspherical lens, which is expensive. According to a special embodiment for use here, however, it is pressed bright and is therefore inexpensive.
 According to a further embodiment, the light source can be designed to emit a laser beam as the beam. In particular, the light source can be designed as a laser diode. Depending on the embodiment, the optical beam shaping unit can have a laser diode. Single stacks, stacked single bars or nanostack laser diodes can be used.
 According to a further embodiment, the light source can emit the beam bundle in a wavelength range Lambda_B and the ball lens allows a majority of the light impinging on the ball lens to pass in the wavelength range Lambda_B, preferably exclusively in the wavelength range Lambda_B.
 According to the invention, the ball lens and / or the optical unit is designed to be displaceable in a z-direction z. For example, the ball lens can be held in a first version. For example, the optical unit in one second version. Both the assembly from the spherical lens and the first frame and the assembly from the optical unit and the second frame can be designed to be displaceable. With this embodiment, a divergence of the beam after the optical unit can be set.
 According to a further embodiment, the light source can be designed to be displaceable in the z direction z. With such an embodiment, a predefined beam cross section can be generated in the case of light sources with an astigmatic radiation characteristic.
 The distance measuring device also has a detector device for detecting an incident beam. Additionally or alternatively, the optical beam shaping unit can have at least one further spherical lens. In this case, the further spherical lens can be designed to direct the incident beam onto the detector device. The incident beam of rays can be, for example, the beam of rays emitted by the light source in that it is deflected back to the optical beam shaping unit by reflection on a distant object. A detector device can be understood as a light-sensitive component, for example in the form of a camera, a photodiode or a CCD line. For example, the light source, the ball lens and the optical system with positive EFL (effective focal length) can represent a transmission channel of the optical beam shaping unit, while the detector device and the further ball lens and the optical system with positive EFL can represent a reception channel of the optical beam shaping unit. Depending on the embodiment, the transmit channel and the receive channel can be spatially separate channels or the same channel. In the latter case, the spherical lens can serve both to direct the incident beam onto the detector direction and, in the opposite direction, to direct the beam generated by the light source onto the optical element. With this embodiment, when the optical beam shaping unit is installed in a distance measuring device, a distance measurement by means of e.g. Runtime measurement or phase position measurement enabled.
 According to a further embodiment, the optical beam shaping unit can have at least one additional optical unit for directing the incident beam onto the further spherical lens. The additional optical unit can be, for example, a lens, a lens system or a mirror. 
 The additional optical unit can be designed to focus the incident beam onto the object plane or image plane of the spherical lens. This embodiment allows the incident beam of rays to be imaged specifically on the object plane or image plane of the further spherical lens.
 An embodiment of the approach presented here is particularly advantageous in which a distance measuring device with an optical beam shaping unit according to a variant presented here is provided. For example, the distance measuring device or the laser illuminator can include a semiconductor laser transmitter or the distance measuring device can include a semiconductor laser receiver.

The semiconductor laser diodes used in laser measurement technology have a strong astigmatic radiation characteristic. Typical beam angles are 6 ° to 15 ° in the SA and 20 ° to 25 ° in the FA.
