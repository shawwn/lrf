 The present invention relates to a laser device for electro-optical measurement of the distance of a target object to a reference mark comprising a housing, a measuring device which emits a laser beam and determines a distance value from the receiving beam coming from the target object, an outlet opening for coupling the laser beam out of the housing, a display device for displaying the distance value and an operating device for operating the laser device and for starting a distance measurement, wherein the display and operating devices are arranged on an upper side of the housing.
 Laser devices for optical distance measurement, hereinafter referred to as laser distance meter, are known from the prior art and are sold commercially. These laser distance meters emit a modulated laser beam that is aligned with the surface of a target object whose distance to the laser distance meter is to be determined. The light of the laser beam reflected or scattered by the surface of the target object, hereinafter referred to as receive beam, is detected by the laser distance meter and used to determine the distance. The scope of such laser distance measuring devices usually covers distances of a few centimeters to a few hundred meters. The known from the prior art laser distance measuring devices can be divided according to the arrangement of the transmitting and receiving devices in two categories, in biaxial arrangements and coaxial arrangements.

The laser distance meter 1 also has an optical sighting device 8 , which is intended to facilitate the distance measurement, especially in external measurements or in poor visibility conditions, when the laser beam 3 with the naked eye on the target object 4 is bad or no longer visible.

 Fig. 3 shows a second embodiment of a laser distance meter 60 according to the invention for the optical measurement of the distance between the target object 4 and a reference mark located on the laser distance measuring device 60. The laser distance measuring device 60 comprises a measuring device 61 in coaxial construction, an optical aiming device 62 and the display and operating devices 6, 7, which are arranged on the upper side 10 of the housing 9. 
 The measuring device 61 comprises a transmitting device 63 with the beam source 21 and a beam shaping optics 64, a receiving device 65 with the detector 24 and the beam shaping optics 64, the evaluation device 26 and a beam splitter 66. The transmitting and receiving devices 63, 65 are arranged coaxially, ie their optical axes coincide. The beam source 21 is, for example, a semiconductor laser that generates a laser beam in the visible spectrum, for example, a red laser beam having a wavelength of 635 nm, or another laser with a suitable wavelength and power. The laser beam leaves the beam source 21 at an opening at which the laser beam is diffracted. Due to the diffraction and divergence, the laser beam expands. In order to limit the beam diameter of the laser beam on the target object 4, the laser beam is collimated using the beam-shaping optical system 64 or focused on the target object 4. The beam-shaping optical system 64 is a common optical element of the transmitting and receiving devices 63, 65. It is designed such that the laser beam coupled out from the beam source 21 collimates or focuses on the target object 4 and the receiving beam coming from the target object 4 via the beam splitter 66 the detector 24 is focused. With the aid of the beam splitter 66, the laser beam coupled out of the beam source 21 is separated from the receiving beam, which is reflected or scattered by the target object 4 and is to be detected by the detector 24. The beam splitter 66 is designed so that it is designed to be predominantly transmissive for the wavelength and polarization direction of the laser beam. The detector 24 is, for example, a photodiode or another receiver adapted to the wavelength and the power of the beam source 21. The received beam reflected or scattered by the target object 4 is focused onto the detector 24 with the aid of the beam shaping optics 64 and the beam splitter 66. The beam splitter 66 is designed to be substantially reflective for the wavelength of the laser beam and for the polarization direction orthogonal to the laser beam. The evaluation device 26 is connected to the detector 24 and the beam source 21 via the communication links 27a, 27b and determines the distance value to the target object 4 from the time difference between the laser beam emitted by the beam source and the received beam detected by the detector 24 Evaluation device 26 with the display device 6, which indicates the measured distance value connected.
 An outlet opening 67 for coupling the laser beam out of the housing 9 of the laser distance measuring device 60 is arranged in the side surface 15. In order to deflect the laser beam 68 coupled out of the measuring device 61 in the direction of the exit opening 67, a deflection optics 69 is provided, which is preferably inclined at 45 ° to the optical axis of the laser beam 68. The deflection optics 69 are designed such that the predominant part (eg 95%) is deflected as a reflected laser beam 70 in the direction of the exit opening 67. The reflected laser beam 70 exits the laser distance meter 60 via the exit opening 67 and hits the target object 4, where the laser beam 70 is reflected and / or scattered. The reflected and / or scattered laser light enters the laser distance meter 60 as a receive beam 71 via the exit opening 67 and impinges on the deflection optics 69. At the deflection optics 69, the predominant part (eg 95%) of the receive beam 71 is reflected in the direction of the received beam 72 Beam shaping optics 64 of the measuring device 61 deflected. The reflected receive beam 72 is focused by the beam shaping optics 64 and imaged on the detector 24 via the beam splitter 66.
 The optical aiming device 62 is provided to assist the user in aligning the laser beam 70 with the target object 4, and includes two optical elements, the deflection optics 69 as a first optical element and a second optical element 73. The user views through a sight 74, which is arranged in the side surface 14 of the housing 9, in the optical sighting device 62 and looks at the deflection optics 69. The alignment of the laser beam 70 on the target object 4 is carried out as follows: At the deflection optics 69, which is formed predominantly reflective for the laser wavelength occurs a small portion of the laser beam 68 as a transmitted laser beam 75 through the deflection optics 69 therethrough. In the beam path of the transmitted laser beam 75, the second optical element 73 of the optical target device 62 is arranged, which is designed to be predominantly reflective for the laser wavelength. The transmitted laser beam 75 is reflected on the second optical element 73 in itself and impinges on the deflection optics 69. There, the major part of the laser beam 75 is deflected as a reflected laser beam 76 in the direction of the sight 74 and achieved in this way greatly attenuated the user's eye , which perceives the laser beam 76 as a sharp point of light. Furthermore, the ambient light coming from the target object 4 also passes through the exit opening 67 and the deflection optics 69 and is perceived by the user via the sight 74 together with the light spot of the laser beam 76. In this case, the laser wavelength components are removed from the ambient light, since they are reflected by the deflection optics 69. The light spot of the laser beam 76 reflected into the user's eye is perceived by the user at the same position in the target object image as the most invisible real light spot of the laser beam 70 on the surface of the target object 4.
 The sighting device 74 of the optical aiming device 62 is disposed in the side surface 14 of the housing 9. The entry of the ambient light coming from the target object 4 takes place via the outlet opening 67, which is arranged in the lateral surface 14 opposite the side surface 15. In contrast to the known laser distance measuring devices, in which the inlet opening in the front 12 of the housing 9 and the sight of the optical sighting device are arranged in a side surface of the housing, the inlet opening 67 and the sight 74 are on opposite housing surfaces. The stabilization system of the human eye-brain complex work particularly well when the adjustment is made as in the laser distance meter 60 parallel to the propagation direction. In the known laser distance measuring devices, the adjustment of the laser beam with the aid of the optical aiming device is perpendicular to the direction of propagation of the laser beam "over corner", whereby the adjustment over corner causes problems, especially for the inexperienced user.
 At the in Fig. 3 the laser distance meter 60 shown, the measuring device 61 is aligned so that the optical axis of the coupled-out laser beam 68 is parallel to the side surface 15. With the aid of the deflection optics 69, the propagation direction of the laser beam is adapted to the outlet opening 67 arranged in the side surface 15. Alternatively, the measuring device 61 may be aligned in the housing 9 such that the optical axis of the coupled-out laser beam is arranged parallel to the normal vector of the outlet opening 67.
